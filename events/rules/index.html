SHARED RULES

What belongs here?

Events must have a clear connection to ESRA, its member clubs or the wider European student robotics community. Relevant technical exchanges outside Europe are welcome when they serve this community.

  • Robotics hackathons, build weeks, technical workshops, meetups, university exchanges and delegations.
  • A real organiser who is authorised to submit, with a verifiable public registration or event page.
  • Clear dates and timezone, location or online format, audience, accessibility or eligibility conditions, language and all known costs.
  • Respectful, safe and inclusive participation. No misleading claims, spam, unrelated advertising or unauthorised use of names, logos or photos.

How review works

Submissions are public GitHub issues. Do not include private contact details or participant data. Reviewers check relevance and accuracy; they may decline incomplete, unverifiable or unsuitable submissions. A maintainer prepares a reviewed event update, checks the resulting pull request and merges it. GitHub Pages then publishes the listing.

Who runs the event?

Community events are labelled with their organising club and location. Listing an event does not make ESRA its organiser. The prominent ESRA Original label is reserved for events organised by ESRA and cannot be selected by submitters.

Keep it accurate

Use the organiser’s registration page for the current details. To correct or remove a listing, contact ESRA Events. Past events appear separately from upcoming and ongoing events.

Submissions and subscriptions

Newsletter signup requests your email and explicit consent to receive ESRA event updates. When connected, Brevo processes and stores the mailing list and sends a confirmation email. Only confirm if you want these updates. Unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter. Signup is not active until the mailing service is connected.

The event form asks for an @esrarobotics.eu organiser address. This initial setup checks the entered domain without verifying inbox ownership. ESRA reviewers verify the actual organiser before publication. Your email is not included in the public event issue. Public event details are reviewed on GitHub. Do not include participant data or private contact details.

Review notifications are managed in GitHub. Alternative RSS/calendar subscriptions are managed in your reader or calendar. For access, correction or deletion requests, see the organisation privacy notice and contact details.
