GOOD EVENTS.
SHARED RULES.

What belongs here?

Events must have a clear connection to ESRA, its member clubs or the wider European student robotics community. Relevant technical exchanges outside Europe are welcome when they serve this community.

  • Robotics hackathons, build weeks, technical workshops, meetups, university exchanges and delegations.
  • A real organiser who is authorised to submit, with a verifiable public registration or event page.
  • Clear dates and timezone, location or online format, audience, accessibility or eligibility conditions, language and all known costs.
  • Respectful, safe and inclusive participation. No misleading claims, spam, unrelated advertising or unauthorised use of names, logos or photos.

How review works

Submissions remain private until accepted by a trusted ESRA reviewer. Reviewers check relevance and accuracy; they may decline incomplete, unverifiable or unsuitable submissions. Acceptance publishes the approved details in the ESRA event collection.

Who runs the event?

Community events are labelled with their organising club and location. Listing an event does not make ESRA its organiser. The prominent ESRA Original label is reserved for events organised by ESRA and cannot be selected by submitters.

Keep it accurate

Use the organiser’s registration page for the current details. To correct or remove a listing, contact ESRA Events. Past events appear separately from upcoming and ongoing events.

Event service privacy

The event service uses the submitted organiser contact email and event details to process and review the listing. Only approved event information is public; contact emails and moderation notes are not included in the public feed. Pending or declined submissions should be removed after 90 days.

Newsletter subscriptions store your email, selected event types, network/club filters and consent confirmation. Your address must be confirmed before inclusion in a digest. Each digest includes preference and unsubscribe links. Unsubscribing removes the subscription record and queued unsent messages.

The service does not use tracking pixels or advertising analytics. Request limits use a hashed connection address retained for up to one hour. Review and email-delivery records are available only to service administrators. Contact ESRA to request access, correction or removal. See the organisation privacy notice and controller details.

The optional service must be deployed and its operational privacy details reviewed before public collection is enabled.
