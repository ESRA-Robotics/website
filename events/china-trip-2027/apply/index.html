Exceptional work. Technical curiosity. A contribution beyond yourself. Tell us why you should represent Europe’s robotics community in China.

Participation requires application review and written confirmation from ESRA. The proposed dates are 14–23 March 2027; programme, hosts and costs remain provisional.

Applications are being prepared. You can explore the questions below; submissions are not open yet.

Share only information needed to assess your application. No passport details, medical information or financial documents. Do not include confidential project material.

When applications open, your details will be sent privately to designated ESRA reviewers through our email provider, Brevo. They will not be published or added to the newsletter. For questions or deletion requests, contact ESRA Events. See our privacy notice.
