A European community behind every connection

BUILT BY BUILDERS.
CONNECTED ACROSS EUROPE.

ESRA brings together student robotics communities across leading European technical universities. This delegation is designed to turn that shared curiosity into technical exchange and lasting collaboration.

Universities represented in ESRA’s wider member network. Delegates are still to be selected; these are not confirmed trip participants or hosts.

On the planning map · Proposed outreach

ACADEMIC DEPTH.
INDUSTRIAL PERSPECTIVE.

The working direction centres on Hong Kong and Shenzhen. These are outreach targets from the planning notes; university access, company visits and the final route are not confirmed.

Hong Kong

HKUST and its robotics community; CUHK’s embodied AI ecosystem; additional academic exchanges with HKU, PolyU and CityU are being explored.

Shenzhen

Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School (SIGS), including interests in tactile sensing and robotic manipulation; SUSTech and CUHK-Shenzhen as further academic targets.

Companies & makers

Pudu Robotics and XSpark AI are on the working outreach list. Broader prospects include LimX Dynamics, DJI, UBTech and Seeed / x.factory, alongside Shenzhen’s hardware ecosystem.

Tsinghua SIGS is the Shenzhen target; this is not a confirmed visit to Tsinghua’s Beijing campus. Hangzhou and Zhejiang University are exploratory alternatives, not an agreed extension.

The journey · Proposed programme

A different perspective on building

FOLLOW THE IDEAS.
UNDERSTAND THE SYSTEM.

Look beyond a finished robot. Ask how components, teams and manufacturing decisions fit together. The ambition: return with sharper questions, practical insights and people you want to keep building with.

Huaqiang Electronic World, Shenzhen. Ecosystem imagery; a visit is not confirmed.

TEN DAYS.
A NEW PERSPECTIVE.

A working outline for 14–23 March 2027. Activities are illustrative; the route, schedule and hosts will be confirmed with selected participants.

  1. Before departure

    Meet your delegation.

    Preparation calls, project introductions and shared learning goals. Arrive with questions worth asking.

  2. Opening days · Proposed

    Find your bearings.

    Group orientation, ecosystem introductions and conversations about the robotics landscape.

  3. Mid-programme · Proposed

    Go from curiosity to conversation.

    Technical visits, university exchanges and discussions with builders. Explore engineering approaches and share your own work.

  4. Closing days · Proposed

    Build the next connection.

    Hands-on sessions, group synthesis and a final exchange of projects, lessons and ideas for collaboration.

  5. Back in Europe

    Bring the knowledge home.

    Share practical insights and new connections with your club and the wider ESRA community.

Who is this for?

PROOF OF WORK.
AN OPEN MIND.

The hands-on builder

You turn ideas into hardware or software. You want to compare engineering approaches with people solving similar problems.

The curious researcher

You ask thoughtful technical questions and enjoy connecting research with working systems.

The community connector

You bring people together and turn an introduction into a project, a workshop or a shared resource.

Tell us what you have built, what you want to learn and what you will contribute. Selection will consider motivation, technical interests and contribution to the group.
