The programme

01 /

TEN DAYS
INSIDE THE ECOSYSTEM

Working programme / Subject to change

Shenzhen / Days 1–6→Shanghai / Days 7–10
  1. Day 114 MarchShenzhen

    Arrive as a delegation

    ESRA orientation, project introductions, a local ecosystem briefing and a welcome dinner.

    TakeawayA shared technical agenda

  2. Days 2–315–16 MarchShenzhen

    Inside the companies and factories

    Meet robotics engineering teams and walk the production chain: development, assembly, testing and quality. Make time for demos and engineer Q&A.

    TakeawayFrom prototype to production

  3. Day 417 MarchShenzhen

    Labs, components and the hardware engine

    University lab exchanges, then the suppliers and component markets behind the machines. Explore how research decisions meet manufacturing reality.

    TakeawayConnect research with real hardware

  4. Days 5–618–19 MarchShenzhen

    Build with Chinese students

    A 12–24-hour humanoid soccer hackathon on Booster T2 robots. Form mixed student teams, deploy your code and compete against the software from FC Isar Robotics ↗. Onboarding, trials and a joint debrief surround the build sprint.

    TakeawayWorking software and shared engineering lessons

  5. Day 720 MarchShenzhen → Shanghai

    Change cities, connect the ideas

    Transfer to Shanghai with time to settle in. Compare lessons from Shenzhen and prepare the technical questions for the next visits.

    TakeawayA fresh perspective on the ecosystem

  6. Days 8–921–22 MarchShanghai

    Where research becomes deployment

    Explore industrial robotics, embodied AI and university research. Meet the engineers taking systems into real-world use, then exchange projects and ideas with local builders.

    TakeawayCompare approaches across both cities

  7. Day 1023 MarchShanghai

    Bring the knowledge home

    A final share-back session, collaboration next steps and departures. Turn what you learned into workshops, technical recaps and new connections for your European club.

    TakeawayA plan for what happens next
