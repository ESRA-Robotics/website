WHAT WE DO

0.2/

ADVANCING EUROPEAN

ROBOTICS

ESRA connects robotics students of the leading European technical universities in a coordinated pan-European network, enabling the next generation of robotics builders to collaborate and secure resources at European scale

3 PILLARS

0.3/

01

CONNECT

Unifying Europe’s student robotics ecosystems across universities and borders.

ESRA connects student robotics ecosystems across borders through:


  • Pan-European hackathons and flagship events

  • Cross-university technical collaboration

  • Builder houses and focused build weeks

  • A shared community layer for coordination, talent discovery, and communication

02

ENABLE

Removing structural barriers to robotics innovation.

ESRA coordinates and distributes access to:


  • European-level funding and sponsorship

  • Cloud and compute credits

  • Robotics software tools

  • Hardware partnerships and equipment access

03

PROMOTE

Elevating European robotics on the global stage.

ESRA platforms and amplifies:


  • New robotics initiatives and startups

  • Member club events and achievements

  • High-impact technical work emerging from European universities

  • Connections to industry, investors, and global media networks
