Privacy Policy

This notice explains what happens to personal data when you visit esrarobotics.eu. It is provided in accordance with Articles 13 and 14 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of Article 4 (7) GDPR is: European Student Robotics Association (ESRA) e.V. Bayersdorferstraße 8 80637 München Germany Email: info@esrarobotics.eu The association is represented by its Executive Board, named above.

2. Data protection officer

ESRA e.V. is not required to appoint a data protection officer under Article 37 GDPR or § 38 of the German Federal Data Protection Act (Bundesdatenschutzgesetz, BDSG), because fewer than twenty people are permanently engaged in the automated processing of personal data. Please address data protection enquiries to the contact details above.

3. What this website does not do

This website sets no cookies and writes nothing to local or session storage. It contains no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising and no social media plug-ins. No profile is built about you. No consent banner is required, because nothing is stored on or read from your device beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver the pages you request, within the meaning of § 25 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG). All fonts, images and scripts are served from this website itself. Nothing is loaded from a third-party content delivery network, so your browser does not disclose your IP address to any font or script provider while you read these pages.

4. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation. When you open a page, your browser necessarily transmits data that GitHub records in server log files. This typically comprises your IP address, the date and time of the request, the file requested, the volume of data transferred, the HTTP status code, the referring page and information about your browser and operating system. This processing is necessary to deliver the website and to keep it stable and secure. The legal basis is Article 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in providing our website reliably and securely. We do not merge these logs with other data. Processing takes place in part in the United States. GitHub and Microsoft are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission adopted an adequacy decision on 10 July 2023, and additionally rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses pursuant to Article 46 GDPR. GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

5. Contacting us by email

If you write to us, the data you provide, including your email address and the content of your message, is processed in order to deal with your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry concerns membership or another pre-contractual or contractual matter, and otherwise Article 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries addressed to us. We keep this correspondence for as long as it is needed to deal with the matter, and thereafter in accordance with any statutory retention periods.
